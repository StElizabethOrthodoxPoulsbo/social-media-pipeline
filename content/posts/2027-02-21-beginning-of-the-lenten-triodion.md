Today is the Sunday of the Publican and the Pharisee, which marks the beginning of the Lenten Triodion, the season that prepares us for Great Lent and Pascha. The Gospel is from Luke 18 and the Epistle is from 2 Timothy 3. Together they invite us to approach God with humility and a repentant heart.

This week is fast free. We also remember Saint Eustathius of Antioch, Saint John the Scholastic, Saint Zachariah of Jerusalem, and Venerable Timothy of Symbola.

Join us as we begin to turn our hearts toward Lent. All are welcome. ☦️

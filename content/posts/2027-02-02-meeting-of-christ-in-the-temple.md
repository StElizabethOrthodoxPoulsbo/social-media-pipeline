Today the Church celebrates the Meeting of Christ in the Temple, one of the Great Feasts of the Lord. Forty days after His birth, the Lord who made the Law is brought into the Temple in Jerusalem, and the Gospel of Luke (chapter 2) tells of this encounter. In this feast, the light of the Nativity is revealed to all who are waiting for Him.

We rejoice that Christ comes to meet us, and we go out to meet Him in prayer and in the Divine Liturgy. Today's fast allows fish, wine and oil.

All are welcome to celebrate this feast with us. ☦️

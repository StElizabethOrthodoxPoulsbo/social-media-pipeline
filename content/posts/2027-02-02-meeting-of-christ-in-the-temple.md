Today the Church celebrates the Meeting of Christ in the Temple, one of the Great Feasts of the Lord. The appointed Gospel reading, Luke 2:22-40, tells of this encounter in the temple, and we rejoice that the Lord has come to meet His people.

Though it falls on a weekday, this feast is a joyful invitation to set aside a moment for prayer, whether in church or at home. On this day fish, wine and oil are permitted.

Parishioners and neighbours alike are welcome to give thanks with us as we keep this feast. May the Lord bless you and your household. ☦️

Today the Church celebrates the Meeting of Christ in the Temple, one of the Great Feasts of the Lord. On this day we remember how the Lord Jesus Christ was brought into the Temple and met there by those who awaited Him. The Gospel appointed for the feast is Luke 2.22-40.

Come and join the parish in prayer as we keep this great feast, and let us go out to meet Christ ourselves with joy and reverence. Parishioners and neighbours are warmly invited to celebrate with us. Fish, wine and oil are permitted today. ☦️

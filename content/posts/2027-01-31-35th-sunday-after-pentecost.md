Join us this Sunday, the 35th Sunday after Pentecost, as we gather for the Divine Liturgy to worship the Holy Trinity and receive the Lord's gifts together. The Church also remembers the holy unmercenaries Cyrus and John, our righteous father Arsenius, Venerable Nicetas of the Kiev Caves, and Marcella of Rome, whose lives point us toward Christ.

The appointed readings are from the Epistle to the Colossians and the Gospel of Matthew. May they encourage us to carry the faith into the week ahead. Whether you are a lifelong parishioner or a neighbour curious about Orthodox worship, you are warmly welcome among us. ☦️

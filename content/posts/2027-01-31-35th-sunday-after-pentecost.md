This Sunday we gather for the Lord's Day, the weekly celebration of the Resurrection, as we continue our journey through the season after Pentecost. The Gospel appointed for today is from Matthew 25, and the Epistle is from Colossians 3, a reading that invites us to live out our faith in the daily life of the Church.

We also remember the holy Unmercenaries Cyrus and John, our righteous father Arsenius of Parus, Venerable Nicetas of the Kiev Caves, and Marcella of Rome. May their prayers strengthen us.

Whether you are a longtime parishioner or a neighbour who is curious, you are warmly welcome to join us in prayer. ☦️

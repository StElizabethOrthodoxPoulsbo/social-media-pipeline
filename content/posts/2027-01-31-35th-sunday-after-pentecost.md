This Sunday, the 35th Sunday after Pentecost, the Church gathers to worship the risen Lord and to hear the Word of God. The appointed Gospel reading is Matthew 25:14-30, and the Epistle is Colossians 3:12-16, which call us to live faithfully in the life Christ has given us.

We also remember the holy Unmercenaries Cyrus and John, our righteous Father Arsenius of Parus, Venerable Nicetas of the Kiev Caves and Bishop of Novgorod, and Marcella of Rome. May their prayers support us all.

Today is a day without fasting. Parishioners, friends and neighbours are warmly invited to join us in prayer. ☦️

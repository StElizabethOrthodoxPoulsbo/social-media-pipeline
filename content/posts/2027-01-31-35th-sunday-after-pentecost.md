Join us this Sunday as we gather for the 35th Sunday after Pentecost. The appointed Gospel is Matthew 25:14–30, the parable of the talents, and the Epistle is Colossians 3:12–16. These readings invite us to consider how we use the gifts God has entrusted to us.

The Church also remembers the holy Unmercenaries Cyrus and John, our righteous father Arsenius of Parus, Venerable Nicetas of the Kiev Caves and Bishop of Novgorod, and Marcella of Rome. May their prayers strengthen us. ☦️

All are welcome, parishioners and neighbours alike.

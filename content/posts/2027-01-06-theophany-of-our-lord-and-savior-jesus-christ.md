Today the Church celebrates the Theophany of Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, one of the great feasts of the Lord. The Gospel appointed for the feast, from Matthew chapter 3, tells of the Lord's Baptism in the Jordan, the revelation of God to the world.

This is a day of great joy and a fast-free day. We invite parishioners and neighbours alike to join in the celebration of this holy feast and to remember our own baptism into Christ. ☦️

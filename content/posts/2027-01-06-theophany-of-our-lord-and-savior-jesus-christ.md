Today the Church celebrates the Theophany of Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, one of the Great Feasts of the Lord. We remember His Baptism, the moment when the Lord revealed Himself to the world. The appointed Gospel reading is Matthew 3:13-17, and the Epistle is from the letter to Titus. Whether you are a lifelong Orthodox Christian or simply curious about our faith, you are warmly welcome to join us in giving thanks on this radiant feast. Today is a fast-free day. May the light of Christ bless your homes and families this Theophany. ☦️

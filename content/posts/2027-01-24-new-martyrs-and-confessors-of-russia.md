This Sunday the Church remembers the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia, a vigil-rank commemoration. We honour all those who bore witness to Christ and ask for their prayers.

We also commemorate Venerable Xenia of Rome, Blessed Xenia of St Petersburg, the holy Martyr Vavylas, and our holy Father Macedonian.

The Gospel appointed for the day, from Luke chapter 18, tells of the healing of the blind man. Come and worship with us this Sunday; all are welcome. 🕯️

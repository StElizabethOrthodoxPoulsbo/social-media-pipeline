On this 34th Sunday after Pentecost, the Church commemorates the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia, who bore witness to Christ. We also remember Venerable Xenia of Rome, Blessed Xenia of St Petersburg, the Holy Martyr Vavylas and our holy father Macedonian. The appointed Gospel reading is Luke 18:35-43, and the Epistle is Colossians 3:4-11. Come and pray with us this Sunday. Neighbours and friends are always welcome in our parish family. ☦️

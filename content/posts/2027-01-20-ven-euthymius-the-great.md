Today the Church remembers our Venerable Father Euthymius the Great. We also commemorate Zacharias the New Martyr of Patra, the Holy Martyrs Inna, Pinna and Rimma, and Saint Peter the Tax Collector. The appointed Gospel readings are from Luke 21 and Luke 6. This is a fasting day on which fish, wine and oil are allowed. May the prayers of these saints strengthen us in our daily walk with Christ, and may we remember them with gratitude this Wednesday. ☦️

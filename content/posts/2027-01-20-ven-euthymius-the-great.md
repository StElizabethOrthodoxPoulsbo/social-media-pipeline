Today the Church commemorates our Venerable Father Euthymius the Great, a vigil-rank feast. We honour his memory and ask for his prayers for our parish and for all who seek Christ.

We also remember the New Martyr Zacharias of Patra, the holy Martyrs Inna, Pinna and Rimma, and Saint Peter the Tax Collector.

Today is a day of fasting, with fish, wine and oil allowed. May the prayers of the saints strengthen us as we walk toward the Lord. ☦️

Today the Church gathers for the Synaxis of the Three Hierarchs: Basil the Great, Gregory the Theologian and John Chrysostom. We honour these holy bishops together and ask for their prayers. The appointed Gospel readings are from Luke 14 and Matthew 5, with Epistle readings from Ephesians and Hebrews. Fish, wine and oil are allowed today. We also remember Saint Peter, King of Bulgaria. All are welcome to join our parish in prayer on this feast. ☦️

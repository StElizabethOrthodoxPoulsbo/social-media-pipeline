On this 33rd Sunday after Pentecost, the Church commemorates our Venerable and God-bearing Father Anthony the Great. We also remember the pious Emperor Theodosius the Great and our holy father Makarios of Patmos. The appointed Gospel readings are from Luke 18 and Luke 6, with Epistle readings from Colossians and Hebrews. Fish, wine and oil are permitted today. We invite our parishioners and neighbours to gather in prayer this Sunday and ask the intercessions of these holy ones for us all. ☦️

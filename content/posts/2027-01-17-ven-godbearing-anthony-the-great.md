On this Sunday the Church commemorates our Venerable and God-bearing Father Anthony the Great, a vigil-rank feast in the life of the Church. We give thanks for his example and ask for his prayers as we seek to follow Christ more faithfully.

We also remember the pious Emperor Theodosius the Great and our holy Father Makarios of Patmos.

Whether you are a longtime parishioner or a curious neighbour, we invite you to join us in prayer this Sunday and to celebrate this feast with us. ☦️

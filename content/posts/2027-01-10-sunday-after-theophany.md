This Sunday we keep the Sunday after Theophany, still rejoicing in the feast of the Lord's Baptism. The Gospel appointed for the day, from Matthew chapter 4, continues the story as Jesus begins His public ministry in Galilee.

We also remember St Gregory of Nyssa, Dometian, Bishop of Melitene, and St Theophan the Recluse, whose witness encourages us in the life of faith.

All are welcome to join us in prayer this Sunday. Come and see how the Church prolongs the joy of Theophany. ☦️

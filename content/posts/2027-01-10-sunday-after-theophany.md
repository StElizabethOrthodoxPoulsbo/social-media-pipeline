This Sunday, the 32nd Sunday after Pentecost, we continue to celebrate the Sunday after Theophany, still rejoicing in the revelation of Christ at His Baptism. The Church also remembers St Gregory of Nyssa, Dometian, Bishop of Melitene, and St Theophan the Recluse. The appointed Gospel reading is Matthew 4:12-17, and the Epistle is Ephesians 4:7-13. Come and pray with us this Sunday, and bring a friend or neighbour. All are welcome in our parish family. ☦️

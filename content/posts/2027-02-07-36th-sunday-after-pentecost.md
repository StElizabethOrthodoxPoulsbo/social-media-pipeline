On this 36th Sunday after Pentecost, we gather as one family around the chalice to keep the weekly feast of the Resurrection. Today's Epistle is from 1 Timothy 1, and the Gospel is from Matthew 15, readings that remind us how much we rely on the Lord's mercy.

We also remember Saint Parthenius, Bishop of Lampsacus, and our venerable father Luke the New of Mount Stirion, whose prayers we ask for our parish and our neighbours.

Come and pray with us this Sunday; there is a place for you here. ☦️

This Sunday is the 36th Sunday after Pentecost, and we gather as the Church to pray, to hear the Gospel and to share in the life of Christ together. The appointed Gospel reading is Matthew 15:21-28, and the Epistle is 1 Timothy 1:15-17.

We also remember St Parthenius, Bishop of Lampsacus, and our Venerable Father Luke the New of Mount Stirion, asking for their prayers for our parish family.

There is no fast today. Whether you are a longtime parishioner or a neighbour visiting for the first time, you are warmly welcome to worship with us. ☦️

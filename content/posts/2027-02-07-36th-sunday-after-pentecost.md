This Sunday, the 36th Sunday after Pentecost, we gather once more around the chalice for the Divine Liturgy. The Church also commemorates Saint Parthenius, Bishop of Lampsacus, and our venerable father Luke the New of Mount Stirion, who show us what a life offered to God can look like.

The appointed readings are taken from the First Epistle to Timothy and the Gospel of Matthew. May the word of God strengthen us and draw us closer to Christ as we begin a new week. All are welcome, whether you have prayed with us for years or are visiting for the first time. 🕯️

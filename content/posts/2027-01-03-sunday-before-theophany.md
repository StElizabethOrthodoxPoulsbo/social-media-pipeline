This Sunday the Church keeps the Sunday before Theophany, and we enter the Forefeast of Theophany. The Church is preparing our hearts to celebrate the manifestation of our Lord, and the Gospel appointed for the day, from Mark chapter 1, begins at the Jordan with the preaching of John the Baptist.

We also remember the Prophet Malachi, the holy Martyr Gordius of Caesarea, and our holy Mother Genevieve of Paris.

Come and pray with us as we prepare for the feast, and bring a neighbour or friend who would like to see the Church at worship. ☦️

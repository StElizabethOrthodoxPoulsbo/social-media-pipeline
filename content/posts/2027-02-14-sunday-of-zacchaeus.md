Today is the Sunday of Zacchaeus. The Gospel reading is from Luke 19, the account of Zacchaeus and his encounter with Christ, and it opens the way toward the season of Great Lent, calling each of us to repentance and welcoming the Lord into our homes and hearts. The Epistle is from 1 Timothy 4.

We also commemorate Saint Cyril, Teacher of the Slavs, our holy father Auxentius, and Venerable Maron the Hermit of Syria. May their prayers guide us.

Everyone is welcome to join us for worship this Sunday. ☦️

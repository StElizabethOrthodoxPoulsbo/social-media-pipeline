This Sunday is the Sunday of Zacchaeus, when the Church hears the Gospel of Luke, chapter 19, and is invited to hear the Lord's call and to welcome Him gladly.

We also remember Saint Cyril, Teacher of the Slavs, our holy father Auxentius, and Venerable Maron the Hermit of Syria. Join us for the Divine Liturgy as we pray together and welcome the Lord into our homes and hearts. Neighbours and friends are always welcome among us. ☦️
